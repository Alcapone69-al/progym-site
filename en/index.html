    Frequently asked questions

    What are the opening hours?

    Monday to Friday 05:00 to 22:00, Saturday 06:00 to 18:00, Sundays and public holidays 06:00 to 13:00.

    How much is the monthly membership?

    The standard monthly membership is 2,990 MT. There are also quarterly (8,490 MT), six-month (14,990 MT, with 1 month free) and annual (24,990 MT, with 3.6 months free) plans.

    Are there prices for students, families and seniors?

    Yes. Students up to age 21 pay 1,500 MT for 1 gym or 1,790 MT for all gyms. Families (parents and children) pay 2,690 MT and seniors (65+) pay 1,990 MT.

    Can I train for just a day or a week?

    Yes. A day pass costs 500 MT and a pack of 10 passes costs 4,500 MT. There are also weekly (1,490 MT), two-week (1,990 MT) and weekend (1,990 MT) plans.

    How much is the registration fee?

    The price list shows the annual membership fee (900 MT), the access card (500 MT) and the keyring (700 MT).

    How do I book my free first session?

    Fill in the form on this page or message us on WhatsApp. Reception will confirm the day and time.
